### 🤖 About Me

* 🤖 **Focus:** AI Engineering, Quantum Computing, RAG Systems & Intelligent Agents
* 🛠️ **Core Stack:** Python, PyTorch, TensorFlow, Neo4j, ChromaDB, Qiskit, Google Gemini API
* 🚀 **Featured Projects:**
  * **[Quanta – Quantum RAG Assistant](https://github.com/oumouna-abdelghani/quantum-rag-assistant):** Full-stack AI assistant for quantum computing, combining hybrid retrieval (BM25 + vector search), Gemini, FastAPI and Next.js.
  * **[Hybrid GraphRAG Recommender](https://github.com/oumouna-abdelghani/hybrid-GraphRAG-recommender):** Movie & TV recommendation engine integrating ChromaDB, Neo4j knowledge graphs, agent memory and grounded Gemini explanations.
  * **[Qiskit Fall Fest 2026](https://github.com/oumouna-abdelghani/qiskit-fall-fest-2026):** Hands-on quantum computing with Qiskit, including VQE, quantum circuits, transpilation and a team QAOA Max-Cut hackathon solution.
* 🎯 **Goal:** Building intelligent AI systems and exploring the intersection of artificial intelligence and quantum computing.
